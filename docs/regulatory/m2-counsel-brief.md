# NORTHSTAR - Brief for Pakistan medical-device / SaMD regulatory counsel

**Purpose:** Request a written regulatory assessment. This is a briefing note, not a legal opinion. No counsel has been engaged.

## Product snapshot

- Working codename: NORTHSTAR; separate independent product and private repository.
- Initial jurisdiction hypothesis: Pakistan.
- Intended users: qualified healthcare professionals in adult primary care.
- Proposed initial workflow: follow-up review for patients with a previously established hypertension diagnosis.
- Proposed functions: organize authorized patient context; make missing/conflicting/stale data visible; later present only clinician-approved deterministic rule results and approved versioned evidence; prepare a source-linked draft for clinician review.
- V1 boundaries: no autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, medication ordering/change recommendations, autonomous treatment decisions, emergency triage, patient messaging or silent/final HIS/EHR write-back. Clinician acceptance is recorded in NORTHSTAR only.
- Current state: documentation/prototype planning only; no live clinical use, no real patient data, no executable clinical rules.

## Questions requiring a written answer

1. Does this exact intended purpose fall within the DRAP medical-device definition, considering the full product function, claims and intended clinician reliance?
2. If it is medical-device software, what classification rule and class should be considered? What facts would change the determination?
3. What registration/enlistment, establishment licence, QMS, technical file, performance/clinical evidence, labelling and post-market obligations apply?
4. What is permitted for a synthetic public demo, a non-clinical demonstration, a supervised evaluation and a clinical pilot before market authorization?
5. What must happen before the product is marketed or supplied to a clinic?
6. What software/model updates, cybersecurity vulnerability, incident, complaint, vigilance and post-market reporting obligations apply?
7. Does SRO 1049(I)/2025 affect NORTHSTAR or only the emergency supply duties described in its text? Does SRO 1450(I)/2026 have any applicability to software-only scope?
8. Do the Medical Device Board 136th-139th meeting decisions or other DRAP circulars/notifications change the assessment?
9. Is a written MDB classification ruling or DRAP pre-submission consultation recommended?
10. What other Pakistan data protection, health-sector, professional, provincial and institutional rules must be addressed for any future deployment?
