# NORTHSTAR M2 DRAP / regulatory assessment plan

**Jurisdiction:** Pakistan (initial market hypothesis). **Status:** Preliminary desk research; no legal opinion, device class, DRAP determination or exemption is claimed. **Updated:** 2026-11-28.

## 1. Regulatory facts screened

- DRAP's “Is It a Medical Device?” page includes software when the manufacturer's intended purpose meets one or more specified medical purposes, including diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, treatment or alleviation of disease.
- The DRAP-hosted Medical Devices Rules, 2017 contain a four-class A-D system. Schedule A states that standalone software within the medical-device definition is treated as an active device and classified in its own right under the schedule. The manufacturer must consider intended use and relevant classification rules; the project does not assign a class.
- S.R.O. 1049(I)/2025 amends Rule 19(3) with an emergency supply-priority proviso for registration holders during war/national emergency. The actual text does not decide whether NORTHSTAR is a medical device or determine a software class.
- DRAP's medical-device guidance index reports a 2026-05-05 update and visibly lists general guidance including distribution practices, reliance and appeals. No dedicated SaMD/software guidance was visible in that index in this review; the index may not be exhaustive.
- DRAP lists Medical Device Board decisions for meetings 136th-139th dated 2026-08-28. The underlying decision documents require counsel screening.
- DRAP lists S.R.O. 1450(I)/2026, Medical Devices (Refurbishment) Regulations, 2026, dated 2026-09-03. Its applicability to the current software-only concept is not determined.
- DRAP lists development of a Medical Devices Module on Pakistan Single Window for import clearance on 2026-10-17. This appears to concern import administration, not software intended-purpose classification.

## 2. Why NORTHSTAR needs formal assessment

The proposed product is clinician-facing and is intended to organize patient context, surface approved evidence/rule results and prepare source-linked drafts in an adult hypertension follow-up workflow. Its intended purpose and the way users rely on outputs may be relevant to the medical-device definition. Excluding autonomous diagnosis or prescribing does not, by itself, establish that the software is outside regulation. Obtain written Pakistan regulatory advice and DRAP clarification through the appropriate route.

## 3. Questions for counsel / DRAP

1. Based on the exact intended-use statement, outputs, user population and workflow, does NORTHSTAR fall within the medical-device definition?
2. If so, is it standalone medical-device software under the current rules, and which classification rules apply?
3. Does the Medical Device Board need to resolve a classification or interpretation issue?
4. What establishment licensing, product enlistment/registration, technical documentation, QMS, performance/clinical-evaluation, labelling and advertising obligations apply before demonstration, evaluation, deployment or marketing?
5. What obligations apply to software updates, model changes, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, hosting/on-premises deployment, HIS/EHR integration, vigilance and post-market reporting?
6. What evidence and permissions are required for synthetic demonstrations, supervised evaluation and any future clinical pilot?
7. Do S.R.O. 1049(I)/2025, S.R.O. 1450(I)/2026, the 136th-139th MDB decisions or the PSW module have any effect on this software product or any future bundled hardware?
8. Which other federal/provincial health, privacy, professional and institutional requirements apply to the actual deployment and data flows?
9. Which current consolidated text, official guidance, pre-submission consultation or written DRAP determination should be relied on?
10. Which named adviser will own each action and what written evidence will close it?

## 4. Evidence package for adviser

- Product brief, requirements, intended-use draft and owner approval record.
- Proposed workflow, explicitly marked unobserved.
