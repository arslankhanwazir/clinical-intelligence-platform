## Permission request checklist

For each rights holder, request written answers to:

1. May the material be used in a for-profit clinical software product?
2. May full text be stored, indexed, chunked, searched and returned to authorized clinicians?
3. May specific excerpts, tables, figures, algorithms or recommendations be displayed?
4. May the material be translated, summarized, transformed into structured knowledge or used to draft deterministic rules?
5. Are there restrictions on geographic territory, users, pricing, redistribution, attribution, disclaimers or derived materials?
6. What fees, contract terms, renewal dates and version-update obligations apply?
7. Who is the rights holder authorized to grant the permission?
