# M2 proposed scope and exclusions

**Status:** Draft carried forward from M1; requires qualified clinician approval. This is not a clinical protocol.

## Proposed in-scope population

Adults in primary care with a previously established hypertension diagnosis, attending a follow-up review. This is the current product-planning candidate only.

## Proposed exclusions carried forward from M1

The initial workflow excludes the following unless a qualified clinical reviewer defines and approves a separate safe scope:

- Paediatric patients.
- Pregnancy and postpartum care.
- Suspected secondary hypertension.
- Acute or emergency presentations.
- Uncertain or unestablished hypertension diagnosis.
- Other complex populations not explicitly defined and validated for the initial workflow.

## Functional exclusions for v1

- No autonomous diagnosis or differential diagnosis.
- No prescribing, medication ordering, medication initiation, titration or change recommendations.
- No autonomous treatment decision or emergency triage.
- No autonomous patient instructions or messaging.
- No automatic, silent or final write-back to the official HIS/EHR record.
- No claim that a generic data-completeness indicator establishes clinical sufficiency or safety.

## Operational exclusions during prototype

- No real patient information or PHI; synthetic data only.
- No live clinic deployment or patient-care use.
- No clinical thresholds, numeric triggers, treatment targets or executable rules before qualified approval.
- No use of candidate guideline material in a commercial retrieval/rule system before rights clearance.

## Approval required

A qualified clinician must validate the patient population, exclusions, scope boundaries, operational failure paths and any future expansion. Product-owner approval alone does not approve clinical scope.
