## Review rules

- Do not downgrade a hazard merely because an intended control is planned; assess evidence that the control works.
- Include foreseeable misuse, wrong-patient selection, stale/conflicting data, unit/mapping errors, unlicensed sources, unsupported model output, over-reliance, out-of-scope use, audit loss, tenant breach, PHI leakage, service failure and unapproved changes.
- Record hazard owner, action, due date and closure evidence.
- Residual risk can only be accepted by the authority defined by the clinical institution and safety governance; engineering cannot self-approve clinical risk.
