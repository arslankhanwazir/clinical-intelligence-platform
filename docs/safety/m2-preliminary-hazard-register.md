# M2 preliminary clinical-safety hazard register

**Status:** Preliminary hazard identification only. Not a completed clinical risk analysis or safety case. No probability, severity or acceptability score is assigned because a qualified clinical safety reviewer is not yet available.

| ID | Hazardous situation / cause | Potential harm | Proposed design controls to review | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-001 | Wrong patient or encounter selected/matched; duplicate or ambiguous identifiers. | Information from another person is used in a clinical review. | Explicit identity confirmation; tenant/site and relationship authorization; show source identifiers; block ambiguous matches. | Clinic identity workflow; clinician review; negative matching tests. |
| H-002 | Missing, stale, conflicting or implausible observations/context are hidden or treated as current. | Clinician sees an inaccurate picture of the patient. | Display missing/conflict/freshness states; show effective and received times; no silent fill; abstain when required data rules are not approved. | Clinician-defined data requirements and validated test cases. |
| H-003 | Unit, code, time-zone or mapping error during import/normalization. | Incorrect value or chronology shown. | Preserve raw source values and units; validated mappings; explicit conversion record; reject unknown units/codes. | Approved data dictionary, integration contract tests, clinician-reviewed cases. |
| H-004 | Outdated, wrong-jurisdiction, unlicensed or unapproved source is retrieved or used. | Incorrect or unauthorized clinical information influences review. | Versioned source registry; rights gate; jurisdiction and effective-date metadata; block stale/unapproved sources. | Clinician source approval and rights-holder permission. |
| H-005 | A clinical rule is incomplete, misinterpreted, misimplemented or applied outside approved scope. | Incorrect decision support is displayed. | Rule approval lifecycle; deterministic implementation; clinician-approved expected cases; explicit exclusions; version pinning and rollback. | Signed rule specifications and independently checked test results; no rules currently authorized. |
| H-006 | Generated summary invents facts, misstates context or cites a source that does not support the claim. | Clinician may rely on false or misleading content. | Structured output; source/fact binding; claim-to-source verification; no unsupported assertion; visible abstention. | Synthetic adversarial tests and later clinician evaluation. |
| H-007 | Clinician over-trusts fluent output or mistakes a data-quality indicator for clinical sufficiency. | Inappropriate reliance or missed clinical context. | Clear draft labels; show source facts and limitations; never label data as clinically adequate absent approved criteria; clinician remains decision-maker. | Human-factors/usability review with clinicians. |
| H-008 | Excluded patient/presentation enters the workflow. | Product used outside its evaluated and approved population. | Scope gate based only on clinician-approved criteria; visible excluded/unsupported state; route back to existing workflow. | Clinician-defined exclusions and tests. |

| H-010 | Unauthorized user or cross-tenant access exposes or changes patient context. | Privacy breach or unauthorized clinical influence. | Server-side authorization; least privilege; tenant/site isolation; MFA for privileged roles; security tests and audit alerts. | Threat model, penetration/security tests and access-control test results. |
| H-011 | PHI enters logs, telemetry, screenshots, demo data or support exports. | Confidentiality breach and legal/institutional exposure. | Synthetic-only development; data minimization; redaction; access controls; retention policy; secret/data scans. | Data-flow review, privacy assessment and deployment tests. |
| H-012 | Service, data source, verifier or audit dependency fails or is delayed. | Incomplete or stale draft may be mistaken for current; workflow disruption. | Fail closed; visible unavailability/freshness; no acceptance without required verification/audit; existing clinical process remains fallback. | Failure-mode tests and clinic-approved fallback procedure. |
| H-013 | Rule/source/model configuration changes without approval or traceability. | Behaviour changes unexpectedly and cannot be reconstructed. | Signed/versioned release records; role separation; immutable change history; rollback; source/model version captured. | Change-control evidence and audit-integrity tests. |

## Risk-rating instruction

A qualified clinical safety reviewer must define the severity/likelihood scales, harm severity, risk acceptability criteria, residual-risk decisions and required mitigations with reference to the actual intended use and operating environment. Engineering must not invent scores or mark residual risk acceptable.
