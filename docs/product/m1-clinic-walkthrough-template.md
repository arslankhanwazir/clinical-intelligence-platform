# M1 target-clinic current-state workflow walkthrough template

**Purpose:** Capture the actual workflow at one named clinic/site for M1. This template is not evidence until a walkthrough occurs and the record is completed.

## Before the walkthrough

- Obtain written permission from the clinic/site owner or authorized representative.
- Agree the participants and observation scope.
- Do not record patient names, CNICs, phone numbers, medical-record numbers, dates of birth, screenshots, clinical documents or other identifiable patient information.
- Use process-level observations only. If a participant begins sharing identifiable patient information, stop and exclude it from notes.
- Do not test the software with real patient data. NORTHSTAR remains synthetic-data-only.

## Observation vs assumption

Keep these categories separate:

- **Observed:** directly seen during the agreed walkthrough.
- **Reported:** described by a participant but not directly observed.
- **Assumption / open question:** not yet verified.

Do not turn an assumption into a product requirement without confirmation.

## Baseline measures

Before claiming benefit, agree with the site on the process, sample, timing method, exclusions and data-minimization approach. Candidate operational measures may include task completion time, number of handoffs, missing-information rework and source lookup steps. No target value or time saving is assumed. Do not collect patient-level identifiers.
