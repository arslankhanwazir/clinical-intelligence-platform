# NORTHSTAR M1 / M2 clinician reviewer brief

**Status:** Recruitment / review brief only. No clinician has been identified or engaged.

## Product under review

NORTHSTAR is a working codename for a proposed clinician-facing clinical intelligence / decision-support product. The initial planning candidate is adult primary-care follow-up for patients with a previously established hypertension diagnosis in Pakistan. This is not validated demand or an approved clinical protocol.

## Reviewer profile to verify

The clinic/institution and project owner must agree the appropriate reviewer qualifications and authority for this intended use. Before appointment, verify the person's current professional registration and relevant clinical experience using the applicable official process. Relevant adult primary-care and hypertension experience is expected to be considered. Do not treat this brief as a legal definition of credentials or appoint a reviewer by name without confirmation.

The clinic/institution must determine whether one clinician can cover the M1 product/workflow review and M2 clinical-safety review, or whether separate reviewers are required.

## Requested review tasks

1. Confirm or correct the target adult population and clinical setting.
2. Review proposed exclusions: paediatric, pregnancy/postpartum, suspected secondary hypertension, acute/emergency presentations, uncertain/unestablished diagnosis and other complex populations not explicitly included.
3. Validate the real clinic workflow after a permitted site walkthrough; distinguish observed steps from assumptions.
4. Identify which patient-context fields are needed for workflow comprehension. Do not supply numeric thresholds or rule logic until formally scoped and approved.
5. Review whether the intended-use wording accurately describes how outputs may influence clinical decisions and whether regulatory assessment needs to change.
6. Review the source register for clinical suitability, jurisdiction, version, relevant sections and currency.
7. Review the preliminary hazard register; define risk-rating scales, assess severity/likelihood, required controls and residual-risk acceptance using the clinic's governance process.
8. Review and accept/reject the proposed clinical-rule approval workflow.
9. Define what constitutes a safe fallback when required information, source, system, verifier or audit is unavailable.
10. Record comments, decision, name, credentials, professional registration verification method, date and signature/approval evidence.

## Boundaries

- No clinical thresholds, medication algorithms, treatment targets or executable clinical rules have been drafted.
- No patient record is to be used in this review pack.
- The product owner and engineering team must not create the medical interpretation.
- The reviewer is not asked to approve unbuilt software or make a blanket statement that the product is safe.
