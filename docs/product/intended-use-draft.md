# Intended-use statement - draft for review

**Status:** Draft only. Not signed. Not a regulatory classification decision. No clinical use is authorized by this document.

## Proposed intended-use wording

The Clinical Intelligence Platform is proposed as clinician-facing software for use by qualified healthcare professionals in an adult primary-care setting to support follow-up review of patients with a previously established hypertension diagnosis. For the defined workflow, it is intended to organize authorized patient and encounter information, identify missing, conflicting or stale information, present only clinician-approved deterministic rule results and approved, versioned clinical evidence, and prepare a source-linked draft summary for review by the responsible clinician.

The software does not independently establish a diagnosis, prescribe or order medicines, recommend or execute medication changes, provide emergency triage, autonomously determine treatment, send autonomous patient instructions, or finalize/write back the clinical record. The qualified clinician must verify the patient identity, source information, completeness, relevance and current clinical context, and remains responsible for all clinical decisions and documentation.

## Important regulatory note

This wording describes the actual proposed product function; it must not be weakened or changed merely to seek an exemption. DRAP's published medical-device definition explicitly includes software when the manufacturer's intended purpose is one of the specified medical purposes. Whether this product is a medical device / software as a medical device, and its classification and regulatory pathway, must be assessed in M2 with qualified regulatory advice and DRAP clarification where appropriate. No exemption or device class is claimed here.

## Scope limits requiring clinician confirmation

- Adults with a previously established hypertension diagnosis only.
- Pregnancy/postpartum, paediatric care, suspected secondary hypertension, acute/emergency presentations, uncertain diagnosis and other complex populations are not in the initial proposed workflow unless a qualified reviewer explicitly defines and validates a separate scope.
- No numeric thresholds, medication regimens, treatment targets, escalation pathways or clinical rule logic are specified in M1.
- The final population, exclusions, user qualifications and permitted outputs require written clinical approval.
