# M1 baseline and pilot-measurement template

**Status:** Measurement design only; no clinic data or performance results collected.

## Measurement principles

- Agree the workflow, sample, observation window, inclusion/exclusion criteria and collection method with the target clinic before measurement.
- Compare the existing workflow with a future synthetic / controlled assisted workflow only when approved.
- Do not invent time savings, accuracy improvements, clinical outcomes, reduced workload or willingness-to-pay.
- Use aggregate/process-level measures. Do not put identifiable patient information in this repository.
- Any clinical safety or outcome endpoint must be defined and adjudicated by qualified clinical reviewers and the institution.
