# M1 non-goals - v1 boundary

The following are explicitly outside the first release:

- Full HMIS/EHR replacement or a duplicate shadow patient chart.
- Autonomous doctor, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, treatment selection or clinical decision finalization.
- Prescription generation, medication orders, medicine changes or autonomous medication reminders/messages.
- Emergency triage, acute-care routing or urgent patient advice.
- Radiology, image interpretation, medical-device control or autonomous device measurement interpretation.
- Patient-facing portal, chatbot, telemedicine/video consult, doctor marketplace, payments or appointment marketplace.
- Billing, inventory, pharmacy operations, claims and hospital operations.
- Physical kiosks, connected hardware, IoT device development or a clinic network.
- Broad multi-specialty agent swarm or unrestricted LLM tool execution.
- Any use of real patient data in public demos, public models, Figma prototypes, screenshots or unapproved systems.
- Any claim of regulatory exemption, medical-device class, clinical validation, certification, accuracy, time savings or superiority without the appropriate evidence.

Scope changes require a new product decision, risk review and (where applicable) clinical/regulatory approval.
