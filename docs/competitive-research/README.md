# Competitive and Market Research Register

This register is for independent product research, not copying. Research claims must be traceable and dated.

## References to study
- ChironAI / MindHYVE: public product pages, demonstrations, feature descriptions, integrations, deployment, security/privacy disclosures and published evidence.
- IHS Smart Clinic / SEHAT PLUS: public website, public demonstrations, clinic/patient/doctor workflows, integrations and publicly disclosed deployment details.
- Pakistan digital-health ecosystem: official federal/provincial strategy, public procurement documents, interoperability requirements, and regulator publications.

## Evidence rules
For every observation record: source URL, access date, exact public claim, claim owner (vendor/independent/official), evidence type (marketing page, demo, documentation, procurement, independent study), confidence, and unanswered question.

Separate vendor claims from independently verified performance. A demo is not proof of production adoption, clinical accuracy, uptime, security assurance or regulatory approval. A product feature list is not proof the feature works as described.

## Comparison dimensions
Workflow coverage; clinician control; provenance/citation; deterministic decision logic; missing/conflicting data; FHIR/HL7 interoperability; deployment choices; tenant isolation; authentication/authorization; auditability; privacy/security disclosures; clinical validation; usability/accessibility; localization/RTL; implementation effort; published pricing and procurement model.

## Our response
Build an independently specified, focused workflow. Compete on measurable evidence traceability, safe abstention, human review, verifiable security, interoperability and customer-controlled deployment. Do not make superiority claims without a fair, reproducible comparison using equivalent test conditions.

## Clean-room rule
Do not use non-public code, architecture, prompts, clinical rules, datasets, customer information, credentials, internal documents or trade secrets from any competitor, former employer or partner. Public facts may inform original requirements; do not copy protected expression or proprietary implementation.
