# NORTHSTAR M2 research notes — 2026-11-28

## Findings

1. DRAP's “Is It a Medical Device?” page (last updated 1 July 2025 in the retrieved page) expressly includes software where the manufacturer's intended purpose is one or more specified medical purposes. NORTHSTAR's intended purpose therefore requires formal assessment; no exemption is assumed.
2. DRAP's Medical Devices Rules, 2017 provide a four-class A–D classification structure. The official rules describe standalone software that falls within the medical-device definition as an active device classified in its own right. The class depends on the intended use and relevant classification rules; NORTHSTAR has not been classified.
3. DRAP lists S.R.O. 1049(I)/2025 as an amendment to Rule 19, dated 11 June 2025. Its full impact must be reviewed against the current consolidated rules by qualified regulatory counsel.
4. The Pakistan Hypertension League's fourth national hypertension guideline is a relevant candidate for clinician review. The PDF's cover identifies JPMA Vol. 73 No. 8, Supplement 7, August 2023, while JPMA's issue archive page calls it Vol. 73 No. 7 and lists 9 August 2023. The bibliographic discrepancy is recorded, not silently corrected.
5. The guideline's final-page disclaimer states that its content is copyrighted by PHL and the Committee of Writers and may be used without permission for academic and non-commercial activities with attribution. Because NORTHSTAR is intended as a commercial product, commercial ingestion, reproduction or adaptation is not cleared. JPMA's general open-access policy describes CC BY 4.0; due to the guideline-specific disclaimer, obtain written clarification/permission from the relevant rights holders before commercial reuse.
6. WHO's 2021 adult hypertension pharmacological-treatment guideline targets non-pregnant adults with appropriately diagnosed hypertension. It is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO, and WHO states commercial use requires permission. It is a candidate reference, not an automatically reusable production source. Its pharmacological focus also exceeds NORTHSTAR v1's current no-medication-recommendation boundary in some respects.

## Research limitations

- No clinician has selected/approved any source for the NORTHSTAR workflow.
- No commercial reuse permission has been obtained.
- The DRAP pages and rules are not a legal opinion or a DRAP determination for NORTHSTAR.
- No risk severity/likelihood scores have been assigned.
- No clinical rule, threshold or expected clinical output has been extracted or written.
